# FacMed Hack

The interdisciplinary research hackathon of the Faculty of Medicine, Université de Genève (UNIGE).

Clinicians, researchers, engineers and students meet at Campus Biotech, Geneva, to turn needs collected from the people who meet them into proofs of concept that can seed research projects.

**FacMed Hack #1 — Digital Phenotyping** · 6–7 November 2026 · [facmed-hack.sparkboard.com](https://facmed-hack.sparkboard.com)

## How this organisation works

- One repository per challenge, named `<year>-c<number>-<topic>`, for example `2026-c03-behaviour-annotation-video`.
- Challenge repositories are private during the hack. Each team may make its repository public after the finals.
- Every repository starts from [`challenge-template`](https://github.com/facmed-hack/challenge-template): a brief, rules for coding agents, a verification log, and a `data/` folder that says where data lives. Data itself never enters GitHub.

Organised by the Digital Phenotyping Platform Committee of the UNIGE Faculty of Medicine, run on [Sparkboard](https://sparkboard.com).
